## 2. Optical character recognition is approximate

Optical character recognition is a statistical process. Characters may be misread, omitted or
invented, and an error can be indistinguishable from correct output — nothing marks a wrong
character as wrong.

Digits, identifiers, amounts, dates, serial numbers, part codes and anything in an unusual typeface
are especially prone to it, and those are exactly the things people copy out of screenshots. **You
must verify any recognised text that matters against the original.** The Software is not a
certified transcription tool and must not be used as one.

The Software uses the text-recognition engine supplied with your installation of Windows. Its
accuracy, its language coverage and its behaviour are determined by Microsoft, not by the Author.
